Battery status for your Modern Mobile Mouse, right in the tray.

4MBatteryTray reads the Bluetooth Low Energy GATT Battery Service (0x180F / 0x2A19) and displays a live percentage icon in your Windows system tray—lightweight, self-contained, and zero background bloat.

Dynamic Tray Percentage Icon

Renders the current battery level directly inside a color-coded Windows tray icon with detailed status on hover and click.

Smart Low-Battery Alerts

Configurable alert threshold (10%, 15%, 20%, 25%) with a +5% recovery hysteresis to prevent notification spam.

Configurable Polling & Persistence

Choose refresh intervals (1, 5, 15, or 30 min) stored locally in %AppData%\4MBatteryTray\settings.json.

Single-File Portable Executable

Built on net10.0-windows10.0.22621.0 (win-x64) as a self-contained single .exe with optional Windows startup integration.
