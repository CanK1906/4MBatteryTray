Battery status for your Microsoft Modern Mobile Mouse & BLE devices, right in the tray.

4MBatteryTray v2.0 reads Bluetooth Low Energy GATT Battery Service (0x180F / 0x2A19) with wake-up auto refresh, exponential backoff retry visibility, and multi-device BLE selection—lightweight, self-contained, and zero background bloat.

Wake-Up Auto Refresh & Dynamic Icon

Automatically refreshes shortly after Windows resumes from sleep (PowerModes.Resume) and renders live percentage inside a color-coded tray icon.

Exponential Backoff & Retry Visibility

Backs off progressively (15s to 300s, capped by refresh interval) when sleeping or disconnected, showing Retry in Xs (backoff) in menu and tooltip.

Multi-Device BLE Selection

Pick Auto (Modern Mobile Mouse first) or pin a paired BLE battery device under Settings → BLE Device, saved in settings.json.

Low-Battery Alerts & Portable .exe

Configurable thresholds (10%–25%) with +5% recovery hysteresis, optional startup, and a single-file net10.0-windows10.0.22621.0 (win-x64) binary.

How to Run & SmartScreen Note

  1. Pair your Microsoft Modern Mobile Mouse or another BLE battery device via Windows Bluetooth settings (and optionally choose it under Settings → BLE Device).
  2. Download 4MBatteryTray.exe from v2.0 and run it directly—no installation wizard required.
  3. Because the executable is not signed with a commercial code-signing certificate, Windows SmartScreen may appear on first launch; click More info → Run anyway to proceed.
Compatibility: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (x64) with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) • Microsoft Modern Mobile Mouse & paired BLE battery devices (GATT Battery Service 0x180F / 0x2A19).
